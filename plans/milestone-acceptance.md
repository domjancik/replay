## Optional sparse pencil boundary — 2026-09-25

Native maximum-pressure strokes have sparse outline protrusions even when their interiors are nearly solid. A dense random-boundary prototype was rejected: it made the entire edge fuzzy and worsened inspected control errors. The sparse signed-offset model preserves mostly smooth edges, solid interiors, pressure monotonicity and source-position stability. It is available only through `--pencil-boundary`; default texture v4 remains unchanged.

On the original maximum-pressure control, column ink-mass standard deviation changes from 0.055 to 0.240 toward native 0.309. Across 36 maximum-pressure controls spanning six angles, three widths and both current pencil tools, absolute outline-variation error improves from 0.2834 to 0.2215 at full resolution and 0.1433 to 0.1060 at half. This metric measures transverse outline fluctuations over the central path and retains raster-orientation effects. These are inspected validation controls, not a fresh blind capture.

The tradeoff is explicit: all-432-cell mean mass error changes 38.1157 to 38.1840 full and 47.0891 to 47.2548 half; mean cell pixel error changes 2.4321 to 2.4407 and 2.0055 to 2.0152. Page 12 pixel MAE changes 43.5978 to 43.6702 and SSIM 0.33163 to 0.33062. Better outline statistics alone do not establish improved overall fidelity, so the refinement is opt-in. No device access was needed. Evidence and before/after contacts are in `artifacts/pencil-boundary-v2/`; the rejected broad model is retained in `artifacts/pencil-boundary-v1/`.
