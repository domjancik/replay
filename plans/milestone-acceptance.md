## Additional texture review and large-page replay

A bounded two-scale texture prototype tested coarse boundary coverage separately from fine interior grain. It improved representative edge variation (standard deviation 1.217 to 2.006 versus native 1.986) but reduced interior contrast (91.66 to 73.61 versus native 96.10) and worsened inspected-validation ink-mass error (120.97 to 149.98). Its lower pixel MAE was insufficient evidence of improvement. The prototype was rejected and production texture v2 remains unchanged. Reproducible evidence is under `artifacts/texture-dual-scale-v3/`. Native exports contain opaque filled vector paths without image masks; two-scale decomposition is a visual modelling hypothesis, not an established native implementation.

The Pillow backend now caches only an exact completed prefix of compositing order, using two persistent canvases for lower layers and the current layer. Active strokes render on a disposable copy. Backwards states rebuild; nonprefix states use stateless rendering. Static output remains independently stateless. Tests cover layer transitions, alpha, white highlights, erasers, textures, Darken, hidden layers and cache invalidation.
