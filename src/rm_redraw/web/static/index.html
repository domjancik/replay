  1. Connect your tablet by USB. Enable USB web interface in its Storage settings and close the notebook you want to import.
  2. Open the downloaded rePlay-USB-Companion.exe.
  3. Set Allow this rePlay site to , then click Start USB access.
  4. Click Copy connection key, paste it below, and select Connect companion. Allow local-network access if your browser asks.
  5. Browse your tablet’s folders below and import a notebook. Keep the companion open while importing; click Stop or close it when finished.

Disconnect or remove saved connection

Disconnect revokes this reMarkable registration and removes the saved connection. Removing only the saved connection leaves the registration active. Imported notebooks and videos remain. Removing a saved connection does not delete its passkey from your password manager.

Privacy and connection help

Passkey and password options encrypt the saved cloud credential and notebook list in this browser. Session only saves neither. Without protection saves both unencrypted. Our server forwards cloud requests and sees the credentials needed for access, but does not save them or your notebook list.

Passkeys require encryption support from your browser and passkey provider. If unavailable, choose a password or session only. A synced passkey does not sync this browser’s saved connection. Clearing site data, changing site address, or losing your unlock method requires pairing again.

Protection applies while locked; site scripts can access credentials while connected. Imported notebooks and videos are stored separately and are not protected by your connection password or passkey. rePlay only reads documents, but its cloud credential can authorize writes.

Downloaded ink feeds the same local replay engine. Templates and PDF backgrounds are omitted. Text-anchored pages may need a separately attached matching PDF. This unofficial cloud connector may be affected by changes at reMarkable.
